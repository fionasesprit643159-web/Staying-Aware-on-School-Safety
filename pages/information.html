How does the app work?

This page provides information about the Shooting Safety app. Please read the guidelines and safety instructions carefully before using the app.

The purpose of this app is to provide users with useful information about shooting safety and experience on how to react during shooting situations through simulations. Once you click start, there will be a map provided and you can choose your starting place. Starting places include: classroom, hallway, cafeteria, library, bathroom, etc. A timer will pop out once you start the simulation. The time set will be random, but if time runs out and you still have not found a safe place to hide or run, you will be considered "dead". The overall goal is to prepare students for potential emergency situations and teach them how to respond safely. We will also offer additional information and resources on how to react in emergency situations at the end of the simulation. Please note that this app is for educational purposes only and should not be used as a substitute for professional training or advice. Always follow the guidance of trained professionals in emergency situations.
